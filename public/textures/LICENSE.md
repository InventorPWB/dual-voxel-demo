## Plants (`foliage/`)

Colour and alpha maps from the texture atlases of Poly Haven plant models. The grass, flower and leaf cards are composed from pieces of them at load time (`src/render/foliageTextures.js`).

| Files | Source | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `grass_medium_02_*` | [grass_medium_02](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_02) | long grass blades |
| `grass_medium_01_*` | [grass_medium_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01) | short grass clumps |
| `grass_bermuda_01_*` | [grass_bermuda_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_bermuda_01) | grass sprigs |
| `celandine_01_*` | [celandine_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/celandine_01) | flowers and broad leaves |
| `dandelion_01_*` | [dandelion_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/dandelion_01) | flower heads |
| `tree_small_02_leaves_*` | [tree_small_02](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02) | tree leaves |
